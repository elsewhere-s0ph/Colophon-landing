Chapter II — The features

Everything an EPUB could wish for

Each feature is a different ruler for straightening your book. You choose where to run it.

01

Metadata & cover

Title, authors, series, publisher, language, date, tags, description. Plus a check that reads the book and suggests fixes: you decide which to apply.

02

Typographic cleanup

Proper quotes («…» or English), typographic apostrophes, real ellipses, no double spaces, dialogue lines with a proper em dash.

03

OCR & scans

For books born from PDFs or scans: rejoins hyphenated line breaks, stitches interrupted paragraphs back together, removes stray page numbers. "Analyze" shows you everything before touching a thing.

04

Footnotes

Checks note references, adds return links, renumbers irregular notes and turns them into pop-ups on EPUB 3 readers. Broken links? Gone.

05

Headings & structure

Finds headings written as plain text (bold, uppercase, "Chapter 3") and turns them into real headings: table of contents, navigation and accessibility suddenly improve.

06

Code cleanup

Removes useless spans, imposed styles and empty paragraphs left behind by Word and converters. The text doesn't change, the file slims down and your reader can breathe.

07

Find & replace

Delete texts, replace with custom rules, match case if you like and — for the brave — regular expressions.

08

Images & fonts

Recompresses images to shrink the file, swaps the cover and embeds the fonts you're licensed to use: the book looks the same on every reader.

09

Spell check

Finds words that aren't in the dictionary and suggests corrections. Italian and English; proper nouns, out of respect, are left alone.

10

EPUB 3 & accessibility

Upgrade to EPUB 3 with a table of contents older readers can still read, language declared on every page and accessibility metadata.

11

Style presets

Save your style, cleanup and search settings and reuse them on every following book. Your taste, repeatable.

Chapter III — Privacy

Your book stays yours. Full stop.

No cloud, no hands on your cover: the EPUB is opened, fixed and saved in the very same place it started from.

No backend

Static site: no servers of ours, no database, no logs of other people's books.

All client-side

The book is opened and rewritten in your browser. Like a calculator, but for e-books.

No uploads

The file never leaves. You open it, fix it, save it: all on your device.

No accounts

No email, no password, no "accept the terms". Just nicer books.

Margin notes

All clear

1.How much does it cost?

Nothing. Colophon is free. If that ever changes, we'll write it here — and everywhere else — in very large letters.

2.Do I need an account?

No. Open the app, drop an EPUB in, done. We don't even know who you are, which is precisely the point.

3.Is my book uploaded anywhere?

Never. All processing happens in your browser: the EPUB never leaves your device.

4.What files can I open?

EPUB 2 and EPUB 3. The EPUB 3 upgrade is optional, and the table of contents stays readable even on older readers.

5.Can I undo the changes?

Many features offer a preview or an analysis that changes nothing, and you only save the new file once you're happy with it. The original stays where it was.

6.What language is the app in?

Italian and English, interface included: you pick one on first launch and can change it anytime. The spell checker speaks both, and proper «guillemets» work in any language that deserves them.
